### Property Management Software for Modern Hospitality

Pinesphere Stay is building a modern Property Management System (PMS) for hotels, resorts, cottages, homestays, lodges, and other accommodation businesses.

Our goal is to simplify day-to-day property operations through intuitive software, reliable systems, and useful insights.

## What We Build

* Property Management
* Reservations & Bookings
* Front Office
* Guest Management
* Room Management
* Housekeeping
* Billing & Payments
* Reports & Analytics
* Automation & AI-powered Insights

## Our Focus

We focus on building software that is:

* Simple to use
* Fast and reliable
* Scalable
* Secure
* Practical for real-world hospitality operations

## Vision

To build a complete hospitality technology platform that helps properties operate more efficiently and deliver better guest experiences.
